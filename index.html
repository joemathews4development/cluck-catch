🎯 Objective
• Chicks will fall from the sky.
• Catch them safely to score points.
• Letting a chick hit the ground means it’s lost!

⚠️ Beware of Predator Cubs
• Predator cubs (like foxes, raccoons, dogs, etc.) will also fall.
• Catching a predator costs you 1 life.
• You start the game with 5 lives.
• Lose all lives → Game Over.

⚡ Boosters
• Be vigilant about the boosters falling from the sky.
• If the hen catches a booster, it activates a power mode.
• During boost mode:
  – The hen moves at advanced speed.
  – Hitting predator cubs causes no damage.
• The booster remains active for 8 seconds.

🦅 The Eagle Threat
• An eagle is cautiously watching all the falling cubs.
• It flies randomly across the screen to catch them.
• You might think the eagle is helping… but beware!
• If the eagle catches a chick, you will lose 1 life.

• Don’t worry! When the eagle appears, the hen can fight back.
• Press the Spacebar to shoot at the eagle and destroy it.

🎮 Controls
Move the mother hen using the arrow keys:
• ⬆️ Up Arrow – Move up
• ➡️ Right Arrow – Move right
• ⬇️ Down Arrow – Move down
• ⬅️ Left Arrow – Move left
• ⌨️ Spacebar – Shoot at the eagle

🏆 Be the Hero Hen
React fast, use boosters wisely, dodge predators, and defend your chicks.
Only the bravest Mother Hens can protect the whole flock!

🐔💛 Good luck, Hero Hen!
