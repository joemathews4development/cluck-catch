🎯 Objective
• Chicks will fall from the sky.
• Catch them safely to score points.
• Letting a chick hit the ground means it’s lost!

⚠️ Beware of Predators
• Predator cubs (like foxes, raccoons, dogs, etc.) will also fall.
• Catching a predator costs you 1 life.
• You start the game with 5 lives.
• Lose all lives → Game Over.

🎮 Controls
Move the mother hen using the arrow keys:
• ⬆️ Up Arrow – Move up
• ➡️ Right Arrow – Move right
• ⬇️ Down Arrow – Move down
• ⬅️ Left Arrow – Move left
You can move in all directions to protect your chicks!

🏆 Be the Hero Hen
React fast, dodge predators, and save as many chicks as possible. Only the best Mother Hens can protect the whole flock!
🐔💛 Good luck, Hero Hen!
