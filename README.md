# 🐔 Cluck & Catch

**Cluck & Catch** is a fun, fast-paced browser game where you play as a brave mother hen protecting her chicks from danger. Catch the falling chicks, dodge predators, use boosters, and defend your flock from a sneaky eagle!

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

- Chicks fall from the sky — **catch them to score points**
- Predator cubs also fall — **catching them costs lives**
- Boosters give temporary powers
- An eagle may appear — **it can steal chicks or even the hen**
- Survive as long as possible and aim for a high score!

---

## 🎯 Objective

- 🐣 Catch falling chicks to earn points  
- ❌ Missing a chick means the chick is lost  
- ❤️ You start with **5 lives**  
- 💀 Losing all lives ends the game  

---

## ⚠️ Predators

- Predator cubs (foxes, raccoons, puppies, etc.) fall randomly  
- Catching a predator cub costs **1 life**  

---

## ⚡ Boosters

- Boosters fall occasionally from the sky  
- Catching a booster activates **Power Mode for 8 seconds**:
  - ⚡ Increased movement speed
  - 🛡️ Immunity from predator damage
  - 💥 Hitting predator cubs destroys them

---

## 🦅 The Eagle

- The eagle flies across the screen to catch cubs  
- If the eagle catches a chick → **−1 life**
- If the eagle catches the hen → **Instant Game Over**
- When the eagle appears, the hen can fight back!

### 🔫 Shooting
- Press **SPACEBAR** to shoot at the eagle and destroy it

---

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| ⬆️ ⬇️ ⬅️ ➡️ | Move the hen |
| Spacebar | Shoot the eagle |

The hen can move freely in all directions.

---

## 🏆 Scoring & Leaderboard

- Score increases for every chick saved
- Top 5 high scores are stored using **localStorage**
- A leaderboard screen displays the top scores

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5**
- **CSS**
- **Vanilla JavaScript**
- No external frameworks required
